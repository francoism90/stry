# Screenshots

A quick look at what stry looks like.

:::note
stry is still changing, so the screenshots may be out of date. The current version can look different.
:::

## Home

The start page shows your videos in a grid with their watch progress. You can search, filter by All, In progress, Shorts, Unseen, Untagged or Captioned, and change the sort order. The sidebar links to every other page.

![Home page with the video grid, search bar, filters and sidebar](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/francoism90/.github/main/assets/stry/home.webp)

## Reels

Short vertical highlight reels cut from each video's best scenes, in a full-screen feed you swipe through. You can like, save or open the full video from a reel.

![Reels feed playing a vertical reel with mute, like, save and play buttons](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/francoism90/.github/main/assets/stry/reels.webp)

## Video player

The video player, with a progress bar, playback controls and Like, Save and Add buttons. Below it is an "Up next" list that plays automatically when Autoplay is on.

![Video player page with playback controls, tags, actions and an Up next row](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/francoism90/.github/main/assets/stry/videos-view.webp)

## Edit video

Videos are edited in a window with five tabs: **General** for the details, **Media** for the file and its snapshot, **Chapters** for the chapter list, **Conversions** for renditions and AV1 transcodes, and **Danger zone** for the verification state and deleting the video.

## Library

Shows every video as a compact row with its size, codec, resolution and bitrate. You can filter by verification state (All, Verified, Pending, Failed), and each row has buttons to view, edit or delete the video.

![Library page in list view with verification filters and per-row actions](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/francoism90/.github/main/assets/stry/videos.webp)

## Tags

Browse tags by type (Serie, Studio, Genre, Person, Language). Each tag shows how many videos it has.

![Tags page with type filters and a grid of tags with video counts](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/francoism90/.github/main/assets/stry/tags.webp)

A tag has a name, a type, related tags, a description and an optional picture. Without a picture, a thumbnail from one of its videos is shown.

![Edit tag window with name, type, related tags, picture and description](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/francoism90/.github/main/assets/stry/tags-edit.webp)

## Collections

The built-in **Liked**, **Saved** and **Viewed** collections, plus any collections you create yourself.

## Transcodes

Every AV1 transcode and its state. You can filter by Pending, Processing, Completed or Failed.

![Transcodes page with status filters and a list of transcodes](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/francoism90/.github/main/assets/stry/transcodes.webp)

## Users

Manage user accounts. You can filter by status (All, Verified, Unverified, Deleted), and edit or delete each user.

![Users page with verification filters and a list of accounts](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/francoism90/.github/main/assets/stry/users.webp)

## Notifications

Finished and failed transcodes, imports and storage warnings show up under the bell. You can show only the unread ones, or mark them all as read.

![Notifications page with read and unread notifications](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/francoism90/.github/main/assets/stry/users-notifications.webp)

## Account settings

Change your name and email, or log out, on the Account tab. The Security, General and Appearance tabs are next to it.

![Settings window on the Account tab, with name, email and logout](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/francoism90/.github/main/assets/stry/users-settings.webp)

## Admin settings

Administrators change how the whole app behaves in the Admin window.

### Application

The site name, default timezone and locale, whether people can register, how many profiles each user can have, and the maintenance message.

![Admin window, Application tab](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/francoism90/.github/main/assets/stry/settings-app.webp)

### Playback

The default caption language, stream encryption, when signed stream URLs are refreshed, when a video counts as watched, and which renditions are offered.

![Admin window, Playback tab](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/francoism90/.github/main/assets/stry/settings-playback.webp)

### Chapters

The default chapter type, and the patterns that match a chapter's label to a type such as intro, recap or credits.

![Admin window, Chapters tab](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/francoism90/.github/main/assets/stry/settings-chapters.webp)

### Processing

Turn extracting captions, chapters and seek thumbnails, and creating renditions, on or off.

![Admin window, Processing tab](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/francoism90/.github/main/assets/stry/settings-processing.webp)

### Reels

Whether reels are created, their size, and how videos of another shape fit the frame.

![Admin window, Reels tab](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/francoism90/.github/main/assets/stry/settings-reels.webp)
